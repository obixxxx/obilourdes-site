What I do

Three areas of work. Most engagements involve more than one, scoped and quoted before anything starts.

  • Online marketing consulting

    Hands-on work with your team on how the business is positioned, what it says, and which channels are worth its attention. This is the core of what I do. It usually starts with an audit of what is already running, followed by a written plan you own and can act on with or without me.

  • Campaign planning and execution

    Building and running paid and organic campaigns on the channels that suit the business, rather than every channel that exists. Includes the messaging, the setup, and the ongoing adjustments once real numbers arrive.

  • Tracking and reporting

    Setting up analytics and reporting so you can see what marketing is actually doing. The point is that you can read the numbers yourself afterwards, not that you keep paying someone to read them for you.

How I work, and what I do not promise

Every engagement is scoped and priced before it begins. You get a written plan, and you keep it. I would rather leave a team able to run their own marketing than keep them dependent on a retainer.

Marketing results depend on your market, your product, your pricing and your own execution. I do not guarantee revenue, growth, rankings or any other outcome, and I do not make income claims. Anyone who does is selling you something else.

If what you need is outside what I do well, I will say so rather than take the work. Payments are handled by Stripe, and every digital purchase is covered by a 60 day refund with no questions asked.
